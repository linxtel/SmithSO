## 1. Event Overview
* **Name:** THERMODYNAMICS B
* **Type:** Hybrid (Part I: Written Test 50% + Part II: Heat Collector Device 50%)
* **Team:** Up to 2 participants.
* **Duration:** Approximately 50 minutes.
* **Impound:** YES (Device + spare parts; must be at room temperature when impounded).
* **Calculators:** Up to two Class III calculators.
* **Resources:** Unlimited notes and resources in binders, books, notebooks, or folders; pages can be separated during the event.

---

## 2. Official 100-Point Scoring Formula

> **Final Score (FS) = ES + TS + PS** *(Maximum: 100 points)*

### Exam Score (ES) — Max 50 Points
> **ES = (Team Part I Score / Highest Part I Score of all teams) × 50**

### Temperature Score (TS) — Max 30 Points
> **TS = (Team TG / Highest TG of all teams) × 30**  
> **Temperature Gain (TG) = Final Cup Water Temp − Starting Cup Water Temp** *(minimum: 0)*

### Prediction Score (PS) — Max 20 Points
> **PS = (Team PE / Highest PE of all teams) × 20**  
> **Prediction Estimate (PE) = 1 − (|Final Temp − Predicted Temp| / Final Temp)** *(minimum: 0)*

### Penalties & Multipliers:
* **Competition violations:** TS and PS multiplied by **0.9** per violation.
* **Construction violations:** TS and PS multiplied by **0.7** per violation.
* **Disqualifying violations:** Touching device/lamp during heating or using prohibited energy sources results in **TS = 0, PS = 0**.
* **Missing prediction:** Failing to submit a prediction before heating ends results in **PS = 0**.

### Official Tiebreakers:
1. **1st Tiebreaker:** Best TS (Temperature Score)
2. **2nd Tiebreaker:** Best ES (Exam Score)
3. **3rd Tiebreaker:** Pre-selected test questions

## 3. Device Construction & Testing Setup
* **Objective:** Collect heat from a supervisor-provided heat lamp to raise the temperature of 100 mL of room-temperature water.
* **Cup Specification:** 9 oz disposable, clear plastic party cup (approx. 3.9–4.0 inches tall, mouth 2.9–3.0 inches wide) filled with 100 mL room-temperature water.
* **Max Device Dimensions:** Must fit inside a **30.0 cm × 30.0 cm × 30.0 cm cube**.
* **Crucial Rule:** Parts of the device may be inside the cup, but **NO part of the device may contact the water**. Nothing may be added to the water.
* **Prohibited Materials:** Hazardous materials (asbestos, mineral wool, fiberglass), thermometers/probes, commercial thermoses/coolers, vacuum-sealed devices, and active energy sources (batteries, electric heaters, chemical heat packs).
* **Heat Lamp Specs:**
  - 100–125W heat lamp bulb (non-tinted, no LED, no halogen, no ceramic).
  - Bulb height above testing surface:
    - **Regionals:** 40–50 cm (in 5 cm increments)
    - **States:** 40–55 cm (in 5 cm increments)
    - **Nationals:** 40–65 cm (in 1 cm increments)
  - Heating Time:
    - **Regionals:** Exactly 10 minutes
    - **States/Nationals:** 10–15 minutes (in 1-minute increments)
* **Setup Sequence:**
  - **3 min Device Setup Time:** Adjust device, verify bulb height, begin predictions.
  - **1 min Water Setup Time:** Receive 100 mL cup with starting temp (measured to 0.1°C), insert into device.
  - **10–15 min Heating Time:** Lamp turned on. Do NOT touch device. Finalize and submit predicted final temp in °C before time expires.
  - **1 min Removal Time:** Remove cup completely; supervisor records final water temp to 0.1°C.

## 4. Part I: Written Test Syllabus (At least 3 questions per area)
1. **Area 1 — Foundations & Scales:** Thermodynamic systems, intensive vs extensive properties, definition of temperature, Zeroth Law, temperature scales and conversions (Kelvin, Celsius, Fahrenheit, Rankine where T_R = (9/5) × T_K), heat units (Joules, calories, BTUs).
2. **Area 2 — Phases & Gas Laws:** Phases of matter, phase transitions, phase diagrams (triple and critical points), latent heat (Q = m × L), sensible heat (Q = m × c × ΔT), and Ideal Gas Law (P × V = n × R × T).
3. **Area 3 — Heat Transfer Mechanics:** Conduction, convection, radiation, thermal conductivity (k), heat capacity, specific heat (c).
4. **Area 4 — Processes & Cycles:** Thermodynamic processes (adiabatic, isothermal, isochoric, isobaric), cycles, engines, efficiency (η = 1 − T_C / T_H), First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics (ΔU = Q − W).
5. **Area 5 — Historical Pioneers:** Kelvin, Joseph Black, Joule, Carnot, Planck, Clausius, Boltzmann, Maxwell.
6. **Area 6 — State & National Only:** Radiant exitance, blackbody radiation, Stefan-Boltzmann Law (P = ε · σ · A · T⁴), and the Third Law of Thermodynamics.

### Mathematical Content Restrictions (Rule 5.d):
* **Allowed:** Standard arithmetic operations (ratios, exponents, absolute values), simple one-variable algebra, and simple geometric calculations (areas and volumes of common shapes).
* **Strictly Prohibited:** No questions requiring **trigonometry or calculus**.
