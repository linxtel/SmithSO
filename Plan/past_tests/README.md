## 🎯 Syllabus Area Mapping
Each question extracted from these tests is mapped directly to one of the 6 official Science Olympiad Division B syllabus areas:
1. **Area i:** Thermodynamic systems, intensive/extensive properties, temperature definition, Zeroth law, scales ($^\circ\text{C}, ^\circ\text{F}, \text{K}, ^\circ\text{R}$), heat units ($\text{J}, \text{cal}, \text{BTU}$).
2. **Area ii:** Phases of matter, phase transitions, P-T phase diagrams, triple point, latent vs. sensible heat, ideal gas law ($PV = nRT$).
3. **Area iii:** Kinds of heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation), Fourier's law, heat capacity, specific heat ($q = mc\Delta T$).
4. **Area iv:** Processes (isothermal, isobaric, isochoric, adiabatic), PV cycles, heat engines, Carnot efficiency ($\eta = 1 - T_C/T_H$), 1st and 2nd laws.
5. **Area v:** Historical perspective of the 8 named pioneers (Kelvin, Joseph Black, Joule, Carnot, Planck, Clausius, Boltzmann, Maxwell).
6. **Area vi (State/National only):** Radiant exitance, blackbody radiation, Stefan-Boltzmann law ($P = \epsilon\sigma A T^4$), Wien's displacement law, Third law.

## ⚖️ Division B Rule 5.d Compliance
- Division B competitors are limited to **single-variable algebra and arithmetic**.
- Any higher-level Division C calculus integrals ($\int P dV$ or $dT/dt = -k(T - T_a)$) encountered in historical tests are adapted to discrete algebra / arithmetic step approximations.
